Fidelity Levels

Fidelity refers to how closely a wireframe resembles the final product. This module focuses on lo-fi paper wireframes.

Lo-fi · Low Fidelity

Rough boxes, lines, placeholder text. No styling whatsoever.

Early ideation · Paper sketches

Mid-fi · Medium Fidelity

Defined layout, real labels, basic spacing. Grayscale or single color.

After concept is validated

Hi-fi · High Fidelity

Near-final layout with real content, fonts, and spacing. Close to the finished product.

Before visual design handoff
Lo-fi wireframes allow designers to explore many layout options quickly without investing time in visual polish that may need to be discarded. Quantity of ideas over quality of execution — at this stage.
