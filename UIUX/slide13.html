Forms & Responsive Design

Form Wireframing Rules

  • Every input must have a visible label above the field — not inside it
  • Group related fields on the same row
  • Submit button must be the primary CTA
  • Show error and success states in annotations
  • Never use more fields than necessary — every extra field reduces completion
