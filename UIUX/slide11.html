Hierarchy & Scan Patterns

In a lo-fi wireframe, color and typography are stripped away. Hierarchy must be communicated through size, position, weight, whitespace, and contrast alone.

F-Pattern — text-heavy pages

Articles · Search results · Listings
  • Place logo top-left — F starts there
  • Most important text in the first two lines
  • Left edge carries the most vertical attention

Z-Pattern — visual/minimal pages

↙
CTA
Landing pages · Homepages
  • Primary CTA at end of Z diagonal or top-right
  • Do not place critical content bottom-right — last seen
  • Logo always top-left
Before finalizing any wireframe, ask: where does the eye go first, second, and third? If the answer is unclear, the hierarchy has not been resolved.
