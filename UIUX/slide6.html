Starting with the User

Before drawing a single box, a UX designer must understand who the user is and what they are trying to accomplish. Design decisions made without user understanding are guesses.

Maya, 28

Urban commuter · Coffee shop regular

Context Looking for a local coffee shop on her phone during her commute
Goal Find the menu and opening hours in under 30 seconds
Frustration Websites that make her scroll past a full-screen hero image before she finds basic information
Every wireframe decision — what goes above the fold, what the primary CTA is, how many clicks to reach key content — should be answerable by referring back to the persona.
Key Questions Before Wireframing
  • Who is the user? — age, context, goals, technical comfort
  • What are they trying to do? — their primary task
  • What do they already know? — prior expectations from other websites
  • What would make them leave? — friction, confusion, slow loading
