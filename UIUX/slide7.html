Information Architecture

Information Architecture (IA) is the practice of organizing, structuring, and labeling content so users can find what they need efficiently — the structural foundation before any visual element is designed.

IA PrincipleWhat It Means
HierarchyGroup related content under clear parent categories
ClarityLabel navigation with words users actually use — not internal jargon
DepthNo more than 3 clicks to reach any page
ConsistencyNavigation must appear in the same place on every page
