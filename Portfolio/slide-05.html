Part 2 — What to Include
The 6 Core Sections of an IT Portfolio
01
About / Introduction
Who you are, what you do, what you're looking for — 3 to 4 sentences, written like a human
02
Skills
Grouped by category: Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Platforms. Only list what you can demonstrate.
03
Projects
The heart of the portfolio. Title, problem, your role, tools used, screenshot or link.
04
Work Experience / OJT
Even part-time or internship counts. Focus on what you contributed, not just your title.
05
Education & Certifications
Degree, school, year. Add relevant certs: AWS, Google, Cisco, Meta.
06
Contact
Email, LinkedIn, GitHub. Make it easy to reach you — one click, not a scavenger hunt.
Projects is the only section that cannot be faked. Everything else supports it.
