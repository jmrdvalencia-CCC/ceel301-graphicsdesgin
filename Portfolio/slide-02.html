Part 1 — What Is a Portfolio
Portfolio vs. Résumé
Résumé
Tells.
Lists job titles, dates, and responsibilities
Claims skills without evidence
One page, text-only, standardized format
Screened by ATS before a human sees it
Portfolio
Proves.
Shows actual work — code, designs, reports
Demonstrates how you think and solve problems
Tells your story in your own voice
Stays with the employer long after the interview
A portfolio answers the one question every hiring manager has: "Can this person actually do the job?"
