Part 3 — Design & Structure
Visual Hierarchy in a Portfolio
Hierarchy Levels
H1
Name / Title
Largest element on the page. First thing the eye lands on.
H2
Section Headings
Clearly separated, consistent size. About · Skills · Projects · Contact
H3
Project Titles
Bold and scannable. Employer should read all project names in 5 seconds.
P
Body Text
Readable, minimum 14px on screen. Short paragraphs, not walls of text.
SM
Labels / Tags
Small, muted. Tech stack tags, dates, categories — supporting role only.
Layout Principles
Use a grid
Align everything to a consistent column structure. Misaligned elements feel amateur.
Whitespace is not wasted space
It gives the eye room to rest and makes content feel premium, not cluttered.
2 fonts maximum
One for headings, one for body. More than two fonts looks uncontrolled.
1–2 accent colors
Consistent with your personal brand. Pick one and use it everywhere.
