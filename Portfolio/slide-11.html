Part 3 — Design & Structure
Mobile Responsiveness
More than 50% of recruiters view portfolios on mobile
📱
Stack to Single Column
Multi-column layouts must collapse to a single column on screens below 768px.
👆
Large Tap Targets
Buttons and links must be at least 44×44px. Tiny links are impossible to tap accurately.
⚡
Load Fast
Compress images. Avoid heavy animations. A slow portfolio loses the viewer before they see your work.
Test on your phone before sharing
Use Chrome DevTools mobile view
Use relative units: %, rem, vw
