Part 2 — What to Include
Projects: How Many?
The Rule
3 – 5
projects minimum for students and fresh graduates
Quality over quantity. One well-documented project beats five undescribed ones. Include at least one project where you can explain every single decision you made.
What NOT to Include
Projects you cannot explain or defend in an interview
Template or tutorial work presented as original
Outdated or broken links — test every link before sharing
Skills listed as "expert" that you cannot demonstrate
An unprofessional profile photo
