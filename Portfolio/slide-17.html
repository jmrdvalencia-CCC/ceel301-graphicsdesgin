Part 5 — Summary
Key Takeaways
01
A portfolio is proof of skill
It shows what a résumé can only claim. Employers want to see work, not just credentials.
02
Quality over quantity
3 well-documented projects beat 10 undescribed ones. Every time.
03
Every project needs 5 answers
Problem · What you built · How · Result · What you learned. If you can't answer all 5, it's not ready.
04
Design matters
Apply visual hierarchy, limit colors, test on mobile. A poorly designed portfolio undermines good work.
05
GitHub is a portfolio for developers
Keep it clean, documented, and consistently active. Recruiters check it.
06
A portfolio is never finished
Update it after every major project, course, or internship. It grows as you grow.
