Part 1 — What Is a Portfolio
What Employers Actually Look For
01
Evidence of Skill
Real work, not just descriptions. A live URL or GitHub link beats "proficient in React" every time.
02
Problem-Solving Process
How you think, not just what you built. Show the problem, your approach, and the result.
03
Communication
Can you explain your work to a non-technical person? Clear writing is a technical skill.
