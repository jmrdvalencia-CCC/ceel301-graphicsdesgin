Part 1 — Types
The 6 Types of Infographics
Statistical
Charts, graphs, percentages, comparisons, trends
Informational
Concepts, definitions, topic summaries
Timeline
Chronological events, history, project phases
Process / How-To
Steps, workflows, instructions, cycles
Geographic
Location-based data, regional comparisons
Comparison
Contrasting options, products, approaches
💡 Most real-world infographics combine two or more types. Choose the type that best fits each section of your data.
