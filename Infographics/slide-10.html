TYPE 06
Comparison
Places two or more subjects side by side to highlight similarities and differences. A clear visual divider (VS, columns, or split layout) signals the contrast.
Best used for
  • ▸Product or service comparisons
  • ▸Before vs. after scenarios
  • ▸Pros and cons analysis
  • ▸Competing approaches or methods
💡 Keep the same attributes in the same row — parallel structure aids scanning
