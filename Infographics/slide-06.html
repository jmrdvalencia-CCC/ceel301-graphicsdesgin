Informational Infographics
TYPE 02
Informational
Explains a concept, topic, or idea using a combination of text, icons, and visuals. Prioritizes clarity of explanation over data quantity.
Best used for
  • ▸Definitions and explainers
  • ▸Topic summaries and overviews
  • ▸Educational content and guides
  • ▸FAQs and how-it-works pages
💡 Text and icons carry equal weight — neither should dominate
