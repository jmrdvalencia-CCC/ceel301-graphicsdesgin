Part 2 — Data Visualization
Choosing the Right Chart Type
① Comparison
Regional Store Sales Performance
Compare total annual revenue across 6 distinct store regions to identify top-performing and underperforming territories.
✓ Grouped Bar / Horizontal Column
Aligns discrete categories side-by-side with a shared zero baseline — height differences are instantly measurable.
✗ Pie Chart
Humans struggle to judge precise angles and slice areas across 6 similar values.
✗ WHY NOT PIE?
② Composition
Store Budget Allocation by Department
Show how a single store's total annual operational budget is divided among inventory, staffing, marketing, and facilities.
✓ Donut / Stacked Bar
Demonstrates how categorical parts add up to 100% of a single total visual unit.
✗ Line Chart
Incorrectly implies a chronological sequence between unrelated departments.
③ Trend Over Time
Monthly E-Commerce Revenue Growth
Track the trajectory of online sales over 36 months to identify seasonal peaks and long-term growth trends.
✓ Line / Area Chart
Continuous X-axis for time cleanly displays rate of change, slope, and movement over continuous periods.
✗ Bar Chart
36 separate bars create visual clutter and disrupt the continuous flow needed to perceive micro-trends.
④ Distribution
Customer Order Value Spread
Analyze how customer purchase amounts are distributed to establish realistic free-shipping thresholds.
✓ Histogram / Box Plot
Bins continuous numeric values to reveal central tendencies, variance, and outliers.
✗ Pie Chart
Aggregates into static categories, completely obscuring variance, skewness, and statistical outliers.
⑤ Relationship
Marketing Spend vs. In-Store Foot Traffic
Determine whether increasing local digital advertising spend leads to a higher volume of store visits across all 150 locations.
✓ Scatter / Bubble Chart
Plots two continuous variables (X = Ad Spend, Y = Foot Traffic) to highlight correlations, clusters, and regression.
✗ Bar Chart
Maps one numerical axis against one categorical axis — two-variable quantitative correlations are impossible to plot directly.
⑥ Geographic
Customer Density by Zip Code
Map where customer account holders reside across the country to determine optimal real estate locations for new fulfillment hubs.
✓ Choropleth / Dot Map
Overlays data onto spatial geometries, preserving geographic relationships and regional proximity.
✗ Bar Chart
Listing 500 zip codes on a bar axis loses all spatial context, regional borders, and proximity insights.
