Part 2 — Typography
Typography Hierarchy in Infographics
①
Title — Headline Insight
Large, bold, high contrast. States the key finding. First thing the eye lands on.
②
Subtitle / Annotation
Medium weight, slightly smaller. Provides context or highlights a key finding.
③
Axis & Category Labels
Small, regular weight, consistent alignment. Identifies what each data point represents.
④
Data Labels
Small, bold, placed close to the data point. Never overlapping.
⑤
Source / Caption
Smallest text, bottom of infographic, muted color. Essential for credibility.
💡 Typography in data viz should be invisible — if the audience notices the font choices, it's too decorative.
