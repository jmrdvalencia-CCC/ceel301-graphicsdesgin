Part 2 — Color Encoding
Color in Data Visualization
Categorical
Different colors = different categories
Each category gets a distinct hue. Limit to 5–7 max — beyond that, colors become hard to distinguish.
Sequential
Light → Dark = Low → High
One hue in a range of lightness. Used in heatmaps and choropleth maps. Darker = more.
Diverging
Two colors meet at a neutral midpoint
Used when the midpoint has meaning — e.g., red = below target, green = above target.
🎨 Color Rule: Use one accent color to highlight the key data point — everything else should be grey. Never use color as the only way to distinguish data — always pair with labels or position.
