Part 1 — Definition
What Is an Infographic?
An infographic is a visual representation of information, data, or knowledge designed to present complex content quickly and clearly.
It combines information + graphic — applying design principles to make facts, statistics, and relationships more accessible and memorable than plain text.
💡 An infographic is not decoration added to data. It is a translation — finding the visual form that most accurately communicates the meaning hidden inside the numbers.
