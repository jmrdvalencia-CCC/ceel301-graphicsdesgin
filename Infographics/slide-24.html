Part 3 — Icons in Infographics
3 Roles of Icons in Infographics
① Category Markers
Identify sections visually
Icons label sections or categories, replacing or supplementing text. Allows navigation without reading every label.
② Pictographs
Icons represent quantities
Each icon = a fixed number of units. More engaging than bar charts for general audiences, but less precise.
③ Visual Anchors
Large icons establish the topic
Placed at the top of a section before data is presented. Gives the eye a landing point and helps orientation.
⚠ Every icon must label, quantify, or anchor specific information. Never use icons as pure decoration.
