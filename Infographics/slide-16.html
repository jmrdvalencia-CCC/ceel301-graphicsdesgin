PRINCIPLE 04Context
A number in isolation is meaningless. Context — a benchmark, a timeframe, a comparison — is what transforms raw data into an insight.
NO CONTEXT — Just a Number
+12%
Sales
Is +12% good?
Unknown — no benchmark to compare against.
+12% vs what?
Unknown — last month? last year? a target?
Without context, the viewer cannot judge whether this is a success or a failure.
WITH CONTEXT — Insight Unlocked
+12%
vs Q3 · Target was +8%
Timeframe
Q4 2024 vs Q3 2024
Benchmark
Beat the 8% target in a declining market
Verdict
Exceptional performance — above target
Same number — completely different meaning. Context makes it actionable.
Rule: Always answer: compared to what? and over what period? Add a target line, a prior period, or an industry benchmark to every key metric.
