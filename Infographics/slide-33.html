Effective vs Ineffective Infographic Design
Feature✓ Effective✗ Ineffective
HeadlineStates key insight clearly and prominentlyMissing, vague, or buried in the layout
Chart typeMatches the nature of the data and messageWrong type chosen — distorts or obscures data
ColorUsed purposefully to encode meaning and guide hierarchyRainbow palettes, random color assignment
TypographyClear hierarchy — title, labels, captions visually distinctAll text same size and weight — no hierarchy
IconsConsistent style, size, and stroke weight throughoutMixed styles — some outlined, some filled
Data accuracyAll representations mathematically honestTruncated axes, misleading scales, pie ≠ 100%
SimplicityNo chartjunk — every element encodes informationDecorative backgrounds, 3D effects, gridlines
SourceData source credited at the bottomNo source — data cannot be verified
