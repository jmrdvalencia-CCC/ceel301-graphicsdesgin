Geographic Infographics
TYPE 05
Geographic
Uses maps and spatial layouts to show location-based data. Color intensity, bubble size, or icons encode the data values across regions.
Best used for
  • ▸Regional comparisons and rankings
  • ▸Population and demographic data
  • ▸Sales territory and market coverage
  • ▸Environmental and climate data
💡 Choropleth maps use color; bubble maps use size — choose based on your data type
