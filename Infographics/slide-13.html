4 Core Principles — 01 of 04
PRINCIPLE 01Accuracy
The visual encoding must faithfully represent the underlying data. Distorting scales or truncating axes misleads the audience — even unintentionally.
MISLEADING — Truncated Axis
Axis starts at 95 instead of 0, making a ~5% difference look like a massive collapse or spike.
HONEST — Zero-Based Axis
Zero-based axis shows the actual proportion — the difference is real but modest, not alarming.
Rule: Always start bar chart axes at zero. If you must truncate, use a clear break symbol and label the axis explicitly.
