Part 3 — Icon Styles
Icon Styles — Choose One, Apply Consistently
Outline (Line)
Strokes only, no fill. Clean, modern. Best for interfaces and infographics.
Most common
Filled (Solid)
Solid shapes, no visible stroke. High contrast, bold visual weight. Great at small sizes.
High contrast
Duotone
Two-color icons — filled base with lighter accent. Great for branded icon sets.
Branded sets
Flat
Simplified filled shapes, no gradients or shadows. Modern, timeless, print-friendly.
Timeless
Glyph
Maximum contrast silhouette. Minimum detail. Best for signage and wayfinding.
Signage / A11y
⚠ Never mix styles in the same icon set. Mixing outline with filled icons creates visual inconsistency.
