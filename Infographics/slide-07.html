Timeline Infographics
TYPE 03
Timeline
Arranges events or milestones in chronological order along a visual axis. Makes the sequence and spacing of events immediately clear.
Best used for
  • ▸Historical events and milestones
  • ▸Project phases and roadmaps
  • ▸Brand or company history
  • ▸Evolution of a concept or technology
💡 Horizontal timelines work for short spans; vertical for long histories
