Composition Critique

"This photo uses ___ effectively. The weakness is ___. We'd improve it by ___."
02
Lighting Analysis
Identify light direction, quality (hard/soft), and color temperature. Evaluate whether the lighting serves the subject. Propose an alternative setup for a different mood.

"The light comes from ___ and is ___ quality. It creates a ___ mood."
03
Editing Plan
Identify all technical problems. Prioritize corrections in workflow order. Specify each adjustment and direction. Choose one creative color grade and explain the mood.

"The problems are ___. We'd correct in this order: ___."
