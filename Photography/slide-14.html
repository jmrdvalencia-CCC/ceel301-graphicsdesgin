Hard vs Soft Light
Hard Light
Small, direct source — bare bulb, midday sun. Creates sharp, defined shadows.
Mood: Dramatic, harsh, high-contrast
Best for: Fashion, drama, architecture, product
IMAGE PLACEHOLDER
Hard side-lit portrait, sharp shadows
Prompt #07
Soft Light
Large, diffused source — overcast sky, softbox. Creates gradual, gentle shadows.
Mood: Flattering, gentle, even
Best for: Portraits, food, beauty, lifestyle
IMAGE PLACEHOLDER
Soft window-lit portrait, gentle shadows
Prompt #08
💡 Key Insight: A large light source close to the subject = soft light. A small source far away = hard light.
