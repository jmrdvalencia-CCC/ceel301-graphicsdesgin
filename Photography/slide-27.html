Part 7 · Summary
Key Takeaways
01
Photography is a deliberate visual communication tool — every decision is a communication decision
02
Composition controls what the viewer sees and in what order — rule of thirds, leading lines, framing, negative space
03
Lighting is the primary mood tool — intensity, quality (hard/soft), direction, and color temperature
04
Three-point lighting (key, fill, back) is the professional standard for controlled photography
05
The Exposure Triangle — aperture (DoF), shutter speed (motion), ISO (sensitivity) — must be balanced
06
Editing workflow: crop → exposure → white balance → contrast → color → sharpen/noise → retouch → export
07
Non-destructive editing — adjustment layers, smart objects, layer masks — is the professional standard
08
RAW = master editing format. JPEG = delivery format only. Never edit from a JPEG export
09
Web: JPEG/PNG at 72–96 dpi, RGB. Print: TIFF/JPEG at 300 dpi, CMYK
10
Composition must consider how the photo will be used in the final design layout — not just how it looks alone
