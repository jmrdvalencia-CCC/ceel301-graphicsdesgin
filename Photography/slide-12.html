Composition Do's & Don'ts
✓ DO
✓
Apply rule of thirds as a starting point
✓
Look for natural leading lines before shooting
✓
Leave negative space in the direction the subject faces
✓
Check all four edges — remove distracting elements
✓
Consider how the photo will be used in the layout
✗ DON'T
✗
Default to centering the subject every time
✗
Cut subjects at natural joints (wrists, knees, ankles)
✗
Ignore the background — it competes with the subject
✗
Accidentally tilt the horizon — it reads as a mistake
✗
Shoot without considering the final design context
