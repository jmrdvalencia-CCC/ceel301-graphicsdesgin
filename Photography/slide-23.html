Part 5 · Editing Workflow
Workflow Steps 1 – 4
01
Crop & Straighten
Establish final composition first. Straighten the horizon — a crooked horizon is the most common and most distracting error. Crop to improve composition, not to fix a bad photo.
02
Exposure Correction
Adjust Exposure, Highlights, Shadows, Whites, Blacks. Read the histogram — tones should spread across the full range without clipping at either end.
03
White Balance
Correct the color cast from the light source. Use the Temperature slider (blue ↔ orange) and Tint slider (green ↔ magenta). Or click a neutral grey with the eyedropper.
04
Contrast & Tone Curve
Apply an S-curve to darken shadows and brighten highlights — increases contrast and punch. Lift blacks for a matte, faded look. Adjust individual color channels for color grading.
📋 Order matters: Always correct exposure before adjusting color — a color correction on a wrongly exposed image will need to be redone after exposure is fixed.
