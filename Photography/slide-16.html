Part 3 · Lighting
Five Lighting Directions
➡️
Front (Flat)
Even, shadow-free. Colors most saturated. Feels flat and 2D — avoid for portraits.
↘️
Side (Rembrandt)
Strong shadows reveal texture and depth. Dramatic, mysterious. Named after the painter.
⬆️
Back (Rim/Silhouette)
Glowing rim separates subject from background. Creates mystery and aspiration.
⬇️
Top
Deep shadows under eyes and chin. Mimics harsh midday sun. Dramatic or sinister.
⬆️
Under
Unnatural, eerie, unsettling. Reverses expected shadow pattern. Horror and special effects.
