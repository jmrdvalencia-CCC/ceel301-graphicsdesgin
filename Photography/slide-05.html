The Photographer's Three Controls
Every photograph — on a professional camera or a smartphone — is the result of three fundamental decisions.
📐
Control 01
Composition
How the subject is framed and arranged within the image — determines what the viewer sees first
💡
Control 02
Lighting
The quality, direction, and color of light — determines mood, depth, texture, and emotional tone
🎛️
Control 03
Exposure
How much light reaches the sensor (aperture, shutter speed, ISO) — determines brightness and depth of field
⚡ These three controls interact — a change in lighting requires a change in exposure. Mastery is mastery of how all three work together.
