Rule of Thirds
Divide the frame into a 3×3 grid. Place the subject at or near one of the four power points — not dead center.
PlacementEffect
Subject at power pointDynamic, natural, engaging
Subject dead centerStatic, formal — use intentionally
Horizon on upper thirdEmphasizes foreground
Horizon on lower thirdEmphasizes sky and space
🎨 Design use: A subject on the left third leaves visual space on the right for a headline or CTA.
