Part 5 · Editing Workflow
Workflow Steps 5 – 8
05
Color Correction & Grading
Use HSL panel to adjust Hue, Saturation, Luminance per color range. Grading styles: warm golden, cool cinematic, matte film, vibrant commercial, black & white.
06
Sharpening & Noise Reduction
Apply noise reduction before sharpening — sharpening amplifies noise. Amount 40–80, Radius 0.8–1.2. Use Masking (60–80) on portraits to avoid sharpening skin.
07
Retouching
Spot Healing Brush for blemishes and dust. Clone Stamp for precise copying. Content-Aware Fill to remove large objects. Never over-retouch — disclose significant manipulation.
08
Export & Output
Web: JPEG/PNG, 72–96 dpi, RGB. Print: TIFF/JPEG, 300 dpi, CMYK. Never save the master as JPEG — export copies only.
