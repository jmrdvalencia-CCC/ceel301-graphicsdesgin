CEEL301 · Logo Design Activity

How Group Formation Works

Follow these steps to form your group, confirm your members, and get your randomly assigned client brief.

Choose Your Setup
🧑
Solo
Work independently. No members to invite. You pick your client brief right after logging in.
👥
Group
Team of 2 or 3 students. One person leads and invites the others. Max 3 members total.
1
Group Leader / Solo Student
Log in with your credentials
Go to the Group Formation page and sign in using your school email and student number as your password.
2
Group Leader / Solo Student
Set up your group
Choose Solo or Group, enter a group or project name, then select up to 2 classmates from the list to invite.

Students who are already in a group will appear greyed out and cannot be selected. This also applies to students who have been selected as a member by another leader — even if they haven't accepted yet, they are reserved and cannot be picked by anyone else. However, if a student declines the invitation, they become available again and can be selected by another leader or create their own group.
⚠️ Once a group is created, it cannot be undone. Make sure your group name and selected members are correct before confirming.
💡 If you chose Solo, skip to Step 5 — you can pick your client brief immediately after creating.
3
Invited Members
Log in and accept the invitation
Each invited member must log in using their own school email and student number. Upon login, they will see an invitation banner showing the group name and leader.

Click ✓ Accept to confirm membership, or ✗ Decline to reject.
⚠️ If a member declines, they are released — they can be selected by another leader or create their own group. They will not be counted as an active member of the group that originally invited them.
4
Group Leader
Log back in to verify all members accepted
Once all invited members have responded, log back in to your dashboard. You will see each member listed with their status:

• Accepted — confirmed and active member
• Pending — has not yet responded
• Declined — rejected the invite; released and free to join or create another group
⚠️ The Pick Client button will only appear once there are no more pending invitations. Remind any pending members to log in and respond.
5
Group Leader / Solo Student
Pick your client brief randomly
Once your group is confirmed, click the 🎲 Pick Client Randomly button on your dashboard. The system will randomly assign one of the 40 available client briefs to your group.

The assigned client will be shown to all group members on their dashboards. This is the company you will design a logo for.
📋 Each client brief includes the company name, industry, mission, vision, brand personality, target audience, and mandatory design elements.
